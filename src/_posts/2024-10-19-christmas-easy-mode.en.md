Christmas is approaching. And with it the question of presents. Grandma,
grandpa, all relatives and friends ask the same question: “What do your
children want?”. This can quickly turn into stress. At least it always did for
us. How do we distribute the wishes? What does our child want anyway?

That was the reason why I built [Wishlephant](https://wishlephant.com/) with my
wife back then. We simply collect our child's wishes throughout the year and
pass the wish list on to every person who asks for potential gifts. Everyone
can then choose what they want as a present and reserve the wish for themselves
on the list. If your child is old enough, they can fill in the list with you or
simply on their own. In no time at all, there's one less thing to stress about
at Christmas.

When creating the lists, there are a few little things you should bear in mind
to ensure that everything runs smoothly.

1. the list should have more entries than the people you are sending the list
   to. This way you avoid anyone feeling like a “loser” at the end.
2. have entries for all price categories. Some like to give lots of small
   gifts, some prefer one gift.
3. clearly communicate the expectation. Is the list binding, are they just
   suggestions?
4. ask everyone to click on “reserve” so that there are no surprises under the
   Christmas tree.

And don't forget: it's best to fill the list throughout the year. Every time a
wish is expressed, add it to the list. You can then simply go through the list
before important events and remove anything that is no longer needed.
