This course is for all expecting fathers* who want to feel well prepared before the birth and who want to exchange experiences with other expecting fathers*.

The focus of the course is on sharing knowledge, answering open questions and connecting people who are in the same situation as you.

Among others, we will talk about the following topics:

* The time before the birth and how you can prepare yourself and your partner
* The birth
* The postnatal period and your role during the first weeks of your child's life
* Adjusting to your new life as parents and what that means for your life as a couple
* Work-life balance and time management as parents

During the first session we will also collect all the open questions and then answer them one by one.

### Course details:

The course takes place on **three evenings**, each starting **at 19:00**. It ends at 20:30.

* 15.01.2020
* 22.01.2020
* 29.01.2020, 

Cost: **20€ per person** to cover the cost of the room and the materials.

Location: Probably Paul und Paula, Richard-Sorge-Str. 25, 10249 Berlin

Please note: the course is held in German.
