This year, two podcasts for children won at the [German Podcast Awards].
Remarkably, one even won in a category that wasn't explicitly limited to
children's podcasts.

The [Kakadu Podcast](https://www.kakadu.de) is so old it could have children of
its own. It’s already considered one of the classics of children's radio
broadcasting. Kakadu completely deserves the award. An absolute must-listen.

I wasn't familiar with the second award-winning podcast, and it's brilliant
that it won the Indy Prize - meaning it wasn't even in a family/children's
category. It's the [Spürnasen Podcast](https://www.spuernasen-podcast.de/). Our
little one has now listened to almost all the episodes back-to-back and is
eagerly waiting for more. If that isn't a recommendation, I don't know what is.
We adults find the way the criminal cases are handled very sweet and
child-friendly. If you have a little detective at home, definitely give it
a listen.
